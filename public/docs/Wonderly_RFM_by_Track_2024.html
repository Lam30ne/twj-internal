What to take from this

  • Manage the two books differently.

    Talent needs a few flagship relationships protected and replicated. Trades needs disciplined account management across a wide mid-tier, because that's where revenue quietly slips.

  • The Trades At-Risk list is the priority action.

    $301K across 11 accounts — Kenneth's, PRE4CLE, Citron, Sunny Meadows, Bellepoint. Recoverable with outreach, and concentrated enough to work through by name this quarter.

  • Talent's risk is concentration, not churn.

    With 61% of Talent revenue in Champions, the exposure is dependence on a few creators. Growing the Potential Loyalist tier is how that de-risks over time.

  • Correct the mapping to trust the dollars.

    The shapes above are robust; the exact splits move with the ~25% low-confidence calls. Fix the workbook's Track Mapping and this sharpens into a real operating tool.
